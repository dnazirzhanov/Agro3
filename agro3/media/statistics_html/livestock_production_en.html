Regional Distribution

Livestock production varies significantly across regions due to climate, terrain, and traditional farming practices:

Mountain Regions

Primarily focused on sheep and goat herding due to suitable pasture lands and traditional nomadic practices. These regions contribute 65% of total wool production.

Valley Regions

Concentrated cattle farming with modern dairy facilities. Responsible for 70% of milk production and 55% of beef production.

Urban Periphery

Large-scale poultry operations supplying eggs and chicken meat to urban centers. Modern facilities with high biosecurity standards.

Market Trends

Key Market Insight: The livestock sector shows robust growth with increasing domestic consumption and export opportunities. Investment in modern facilities and breeding programs has improved productivity significantly.
