Livestock Industry Report - 2024

Executive Summary

The livestock sector continues to be a vital component of our agricultural economy. This report analyzes trends in cattle, sheep, goat, and poultry populations, along with production metrics and market developments for 2024.

Livestock Population Overview

Key Statistics:
  • Total cattle population: 2.3 million head (+8% from 2023)
  • Sheep and goats: 4.1 million head (+5% from 2023)
  • Poultry: 15.2 million birds (+12% from 2023)

Health and Welfare Improvements

Significant investments in animal health and welfare have contributed to improved productivity:

  • Vaccination coverage increased to 98% across all livestock categories
  • Implementation of digital health monitoring systems
  • Enhanced feed quality standards resulting in better growth rates
  • Improved housing conditions reducing mortality rates by 15%

Market Outlook

The livestock sector shows strong potential for continued growth, supported by increasing domestic demand and export opportunities. Key factors driving this optimism include:

  1. Growing middle-class population demanding higher-quality protein
  2. Government support for modernization initiatives
  3. Development of processing facilities improving value addition
  4. Sustainable farming practices attracting premium markets
